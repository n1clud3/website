### Why Gmod?

This game is the only one which makes me feel something when developing.
I've tried Godot, but it felt... easy? Too boring for me? I guess I'd rather fight
gazillion gmod lua and source engine bugs and quirks than build a game.
That makes me feel something. For now, I haven't found impassable walls which would break
my gameplay ideas. And I don't have to make original assets. HL2 is already good. All I need
is to edit some assets to suit the needs and it's gonna be fire. Better than using GenAI and shit.

I plan to make original assets! Just not now. Not in this decade...
